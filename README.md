## Notes

- Estimates vary widely across different historical sources due to differences in methodology, record-keeping limitations during wartime, and ongoing scholarly research.
- The Soviet Union and China suffered by far the highest total casualties in the war.
- Civilian deaths include deaths from military action, war crimes, genocide (such as the Holocaust), famine, and disease caused by war.
- The Holocaust accounted for approximately 6 million Jewish deaths, primarily counted in the civilian figures for Poland, Soviet Union, and other European nations.
