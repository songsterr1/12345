# Richard Kruspe and Rammstein Members

## Is Richard Kruspe American?

No, Richard Kruspe (full name: Richard Zven Kruspe, born June 24, 1967) is **German**, not American. He was born in Wittenberge, East Germany (German Democratic Republic). He is best known as the lead guitarist and one of the founding members of the industrial metal band **Rammstein**. While he has spent time living in the United States and has cited American musical influences, he holds German nationality.

## Rammstein Band Members

All members of Rammstein are **German**. The band was formed in Berlin, Germany in 1994, and the lineup has remained unchanged since its formation. All six members were born in what was then East Germany:
