# Competitive Strength Analysis: 油污净 (Oil Stain Cleaning) Industry

---

## 1. 广州立白企业集团有限公司 (Guangzhou Libai Group Co., Ltd.)

Guangzhou Libai Group is one of China's largest and most established domestic daily chemical enterprises, with a registered capital of RMB 800 million and decades of operational history. In the oil stain cleaning (油污净) segment, Libai holds several notable competitive advantages.

**Brand Recognition & Market Penetration:** Libai has cultivated a strong household brand presence across China, particularly in lower-tier cities and rural markets where trust in domestic brands runs high. This widespread consumer familiarity gives Libai a significant distribution edge in the 油污净 category.

**Comprehensive Product Portfolio:** Libai's manufacturing capabilities span synthetic detergents, soaps, and other household chemical products, enabling it to cross-sell and bundle oil stain cleaning solutions alongside its core laundry and cleaning lineup. This breadth reinforces shelf presence and retail leverage.

**Integrated R&D and Manufacturing:** With in-house research capabilities in materials science and chemical engineering, Libai can develop and iterate on oil-cutting formulations efficiently, reducing time-to-market for new 油污净 products.

**Extensive Distribution Network:** Operating through both traditional wholesale channels and modern retail, Libai's nationwide logistics infrastructure ensures broad availability of its oil stain cleaning products, including in markets underserved by premium competitors.

**Cost Competitiveness:** As a large-scale domestic manufacturer, Libai benefits from economies of scale in raw material procurement and production, allowing competitive pricing that appeals to cost-conscious Chinese consumers in the mass-market segment.

## 2. 蓝月亮（中国）有限公司 (Blue Moon (China) Co., Ltd.)

Blue Moon is a Hong Kong-invested enterprise with a registered capital of USD 170 million, widely recognized for premium household cleaning products. In the 油污净 industry, Blue Moon demonstrates distinct competitive strengths.

**Premium Brand Positioning:** Blue Moon has successfully cultivated a reputation for high-efficacy, science-backed cleaning products. Its branding resonates with urban, middle-class consumers who prioritize cleaning performance and ingredient safety, commanding a price premium in the 油污净 category.

**Innovation-Driven Product Development:** With strong R&D investment in engineering, new materials, and chemical research, Blue Moon continually advances its oil stain removal formulas. This commitment to innovation helps differentiate its products in an increasingly competitive market.

**E-commerce & Digital Leadership:** Blue Moon was an early mover in online retail and live-streaming commerce, establishing a dominant presence on platforms such as Tmall and JD.com. Its ability to leverage digital sales channels provides direct consumer access and reduces dependence on traditional distribution intermediaries.

**Diversified Service Offerings:** Beyond product sales, Blue Moon has expanded into laundry services, professional cleaning, and home services, creating an ecosystem around its core oil stain cleaning products. This diversification strengthens customer loyalty and opens recurring revenue streams.

**International Capital Backing:** As a Hong Kong-invested entity, Blue Moon benefits from access to international capital markets and global best practices in R&D and marketing, supporting sustained investment in product quality and brand building within China's competitive cleaning products landscape.
