## Competitive Strength Analysis: 油污净 (Oil Stain Cleaning) Industry

---

### 1. Guangzhou Liby Enterprise Group Co., Ltd. (广州立白企业集团有限公司)

Guangzhou Liby Enterprise Group is one of China's most prominent domestic daily chemical conglomerates, with a registered capital of RMB 800 million and decades of brand-building in the cleaning products sector. In the oil stain cleaning (油污净) segment, Liby's competitive strengths are multifaceted.

**Brand Equity and Market Penetration:** Liby has cultivated strong consumer recognition across tier-1 through tier-4 cities in China. Its extensive retail network and long-standing relationships with both offline supermarket chains and online e-commerce platforms give it unrivaled shelf presence in the oil stain cleaning category.

**Vertically Integrated Manufacturing:** With licensed operations spanning soap and synthetic detergent manufacturing, as well as other daily chemical product manufacturing, Liby controls critical steps of the value chain. This vertical integration enables cost efficiency and rapid product iteration—key advantages in a price-sensitive commodity segment like oil stain cleaners.

**R&D and Innovation Capability:** The company's business scope explicitly includes natural science R&D and engineering and technology research, reflecting meaningful investment in formulation science. This supports the development of advanced degreasing agents that tackle stubborn kitchen oil stains while meeting increasingly stringent environmental standards.

**Distribution and Logistics Strength:** Liby's nationwide distribution infrastructure, backed by its own property assets and warehousing capabilities, ensures product availability and supply-chain resilience—a decisive advantage during demand surges or supply disruptions.

**Corporate Scale and Financial Resources:** With a large registered capital and diversified revenue streams (including advertising, property leasing, and investment consulting), Liby has the financial staying power to invest in marketing campaigns and promotional pricing strategies that smaller rivals cannot match.

Overall, Liby's combination of brand heritage, manufacturing scale, R&D investment, and distribution depth positions it as a leading force in the domestic oil stain cleaning market.

---

### 2. Blue Moon (China) Co., Ltd. (蓝月亮（中国）有限公司)

Blue Moon (China) Co., Ltd. is a Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan-funded enterprise with a registered capital exceeding USD 170 million, making it one of the most well-capitalised players in China's daily chemical industry. In the oil stain cleaning (油污净) segment, Blue Moon leverages several distinctive competitive strengths.

**Premium Positioning and Consumer Trust:** Blue Moon has built a reputation for high-efficacy, science-backed cleaning formulations. Its brand is associated with quality and innovation, commanding premium price points that generate higher per-unit margins in the oil stain cleaning category.

**Advanced Product Formulation and Technology:** The company's broad R&D mandate—covering engineering and technology research, new materials technology, and chemical product production—underpins continuous formulation advancement. Blue Moon's cleaning products are known for concentrated, more environmentally friendly formulas, a growing differentiator as consumers become more eco-conscious.

**Omnichannel Retail and Digital Commerce Strength:** Blue Moon has demonstrated sophistication in e-commerce and live-streaming commerce (its business scope includes individual internet live-streaming services), having achieved landmark sales volumes on platforms like JD.com and Tmall. This digital-first capability is critical in reaching younger, urban consumers who purchase household cleaning products online.

**Comprehensive Product Portfolio:** Beyond oil stain cleaners, Blue Moon's integrated product ecosystem—spanning laundry detergents, hand soaps, and disinfectants—enables cross-selling and promotional bundling, reinforcing consumer loyalty within the cleaning category.

**International Ownership Advantages:** As a Hong Kong-invested entity, Blue Moon benefits from international management practices, access to global best-in-class technology and ingredients, and a governance structure that facilitates long-term capital allocation toward innovation and brand building.

In summary, Blue Moon's competitive edge in the oil stain cleaning market rests on its premium brand positioning, technology-driven formulations, powerful digital marketing capabilities, and strong financial foundation—making it a formidable challenger to domestic incumbents.
