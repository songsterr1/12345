## 通用打印耗材 (General Printing Consumables) — Industry Definition

**通用打印耗材** (tōngyòng dǎyìn hàocái) refers to generic or compatible printing consumables — such as ink cartridges, toner cartridges, printer ribbons, and related supplies — that are manufactured by third-party producers to be interchangeable with the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) products. These consumables are designed to function in a wide range of printer models from multiple brands without being tied to a single manufacturer.

Key characteristics of the industry include:
- **Compatibility**: Products are engineered to meet or exceed the performance specifications of OEM counterparts.
- **Cost-effectiveness**: Generic consumables are typically sold at a lower price point than branded originals.
- **Wide applicability**: A single product SKU may be compatible with numerous printer models across different brands (e.g., HP, Canon, Epson, Brother).
- **Market scope**: The industry encompasses procurement, manufacturing, distribution, and resale of ink, toner, drum units, fuser assemblies, and other replaceable printer components.

## Cloud Mode — Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why can't cloud mode generate responses for some questions?**

Cloud mode relies on an external AI service endpoint. Responses may not be generated when:
1. The cloud service endpoint is unreachable or experiencing downtime.
2. The request exceeds the token budget or rate limits imposed by the cloud provider.
3. The question contains content that is filtered by the cloud service's safety policies.
4. Network connectivity issues prevent the request from reaching the remote service.

If cloud mode is unavailable, switching to a local model or retrying after a short interval is recommended.
