# Competitive Strength Analysis: Oil Stain Cleaning (油污净) Industry

## 1. 广州立白企业集团有限公司 (Guangzhou Libai Enterprise Group Co., Ltd.)

**Competitive Strength Analysis (~300 words)**

Guangzhou Libai Enterprise Group Co., Ltd. stands as one of China's most formidable domestic cleaning product manufacturers, boasting a registered capital of RMB 800 million. In the oil stain cleaning (油污净) segment, Libai leverages several key competitive advantages.

**Brand Power & Market Penetration:** Libai is one of the most recognized household cleaning brands in China, with decades of brand-building experience. Its mass-market brand recognition gives it strong shelf presence in supermarkets, convenience stores, and e-commerce platforms nationwide, enabling effective distribution of oil-cleaning products to both urban and rural consumers.

**Vertical Integration & Manufacturing Scale:** With a business scope covering soap and synthetic detergent manufacturing, other daily chemical products, and raw material R&D, Libai controls a significant portion of its supply chain. This integration reduces costs and enables faster product iteration in competitive segments like kitchen degreasing products.

**R&D Capability:** Libai invests in natural science research, engineering technology R&D, chemical engineering research, and materials science—all directly applicable to developing advanced oil-stain-removing formulations. This scientific foundation helps differentiate its products from lower-cost competitors.

**Diverse Distribution & Marketing:** The company operates across wholesale, retail, import/export, and digital marketing channels, with explicit capabilities in advertising and marketing strategy. Its omnichannel presence allows it to compete effectively in both traditional offline retail and growing online marketplaces.

**Operational Strength:** Corporate headquarters management, enterprise management services, and property management capabilities underpin an efficient, large-scale operation that can sustain competitive pricing and promotional activity.

**Limitations:** As an unlisted (non-public) company, Libai has comparatively constrained access to capital markets versus publicly traded competitors, which may limit the pace of large-scale expansion or heavy R&D investment.

## 2. 蓝月亮（中国）有限公司 (Blue Moon (China) Co., Ltd.)

**Competitive Strength Analysis (~300 words)**

Blue Moon (China) Co., Ltd., with a registered capital of approximately USD 170 million, is a leading player in the household cleaning and laundry products segment. In the oil stain cleaning (油污净) category, Blue Moon presents a distinct competitive profile:

**Premium Brand & Innovation Leadership:** Blue Moon is widely recognized as a premium cleaning brand in China, particularly for its high-performance laundry and stain-removal products. Its brand equity commands consumer willingness to pay a premium, a critical advantage in the oil stain cleaning market where product efficacy drives purchase decisions.

**Technology-Driven Formulation:** The company actively invests in engineering and technology research, new materials technology promotion, and technical services including development, consulting, and transfer. This R&D orientation supports cutting-edge oil-dissolving and degreasing formulations that differentiate Blue Moon products from commodity competitors.

**Manufacturing & Quality Control:** Blue Moon holds licenses for daily chemical product manufacturing, food-grade detergent production, cosmetics production, and disinfectant manufacturing—reflecting robust regulatory compliance and high-quality manufacturing standards. For kitchen and oil-stain cleaners, certified safe-for-food-contact formulations are a significant competitive plus.

**Digital & Direct-to-Consumer Strength:** With capabilities spanning e-commerce, individual internet live-streaming services, digital content creation, data processing, and online transaction processing, Blue Moon has built strong direct-to-consumer digital channels. This is particularly valuable as online retail becomes the primary battleground for cleaning product sales in China.

**Foreign Capital Backing:** As a Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan-invested entity, Blue Moon benefits from access to international capital, global best practices, and potentially advanced overseas technology partnerships.

**Logistics & After-Sales:** Capabilities in warehousing, cargo packaging, laundry services, and professional cleaning services allow Blue Moon to extend its value proposition beyond product sales into service solutions.

**Limitations:** Blue Moon's premium positioning may limit its penetration in price-sensitive rural markets compared to more value-oriented domestic competitors.
