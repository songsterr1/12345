Dimension 1 — 销量 Sales Volume  [单一类指标 / Single Indicator]
  Basis: 2024 actual sales volume (万支/10,000 PCS), scaled proportionally
  to 100 (maximum = 2,500万支 = 100 points).
  Rationale: Objective, directly comparable, no subjectivity.

Dimension 2 — 市场力 Market Power  [复合类指标 / Composite Indicator]
  Components: Market share (市占率) + Revenue base (营收) + Customer coverage (客户情况)
  Scoring basis: G&G holds the global #1 market share (~2.3% market share per report);
  Dinglong and Print-Rite have broad B2B customer networks; others are
  mid-tier participants.

Dimension 3 — 创新力 Innovation  [复合类指标 / Composite Indicator]
  Components: R&D capability (技术能力) + Technical patents (技术专利)
  Scoring basis: G&G leads in smart-chip cartridge R&D;
  Dinglong is a listed company with significant disclosed R&D expenditure;
  Print-Rite (Zhuhai Tianwei Feima) has decades of OPC drum / photoconductor
  specialty engineering heritage.

Dimension 4 — 品牌力 Brand Strength  [复合类指标 / Composite Indicator]
  Components: Global brand recognition + Distribution channel breadth
  Scoring basis: G&G (G&G brand) = top-tier global generic brand;
  Print-Rite has long-established international brand presence (Print-Rite brand);
  Dinglong is a recognized publicly-listed company in China.

■  KEY FINDINGS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• 珠海格之格 dominates on all four dimensions, especially Sales Volume (100)
  and Market Power (95) — consistent with its global #1 market share position.

• 湖北鼎龙 and 珠海天威飞马 show a distinctive profile: despite lower sales
  volumes vs. top manufacturers, they score significantly higher on Innovation
  and Brand Strength — indicating depth-over-volume competitive strategies.

• 珠海天威飞马 exhibits a "low sales / high brand" profile (32 sales, 78 brand),
  reflecting its long heritage as an international OEM/brand specialist
  (OPC drum photoconductor technology) rather than a volume-driven model.

• 江西亿铂 and 珠海中凯 are volume-driven mid-tier players with balanced but
  undifferentiated profiles across Market Power, Innovation, and Brand.

• 珠海美景联合 trails across all four dimensions, indicating limited
  differentiation and reliance on OEM/ODM contract manufacturing.
