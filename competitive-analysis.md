# Competitive Strength Analysis: 油污净 (Oil Stain Removal) Industry

## 广州立白企业集团有限公司 (Guangzhou Libai Enterprise Group Co., Ltd.)

Guangzhou Libai Enterprise Group is one of China's most established domestic household cleaning product manufacturers, with a registered capital of RMB 800 million. In the 油污净 (oil stain removal) industry, the company holds several notable competitive advantages.

**Brand Equity and Market Penetration:** Libai is a household name across China, particularly in lower-tier cities and rural markets. Its decades-long presence in the daily chemical industry has cultivated deep consumer trust, which translates directly into repeat purchases in the oil stain removal segment. The brand's mass-market positioning allows it to compete aggressively on price while maintaining broad retail reach through an extensive offline distribution network spanning hypermarkets, convenience stores, and e-commerce platforms.

**Integrated Manufacturing Capability:** With a business scope that includes soap and synthetic detergent manufacturing, as well as other daily chemical products, Libai possesses vertically integrated production lines. This enables cost efficiency in sourcing raw materials and managing production for oil-removal formulations, giving it a pricing advantage over competitors relying on third-party manufacturers.

**R&D and Innovation Infrastructure:** Libai's registered scope includes natural science research, engineering and technology research, and chemical engineering research services. This internal R&D capability supports the development of advanced oil-dissolving formulations, allowing the company to respond to evolving consumer demands for more effective and eco-friendly oil stain removers.

**Diversified Distribution Channels:** Libai's operations span wholesale, retail, and e-commerce (permitted value-added telecom services), ensuring its oil stain removal products reach consumers across all major purchasing channels. Its large-scale logistics and sales network provides a competitive edge in product availability and speed-to-market.

**Corporate Backing and Scale:** As a major enterprise group headquartered in Guangzhou, Libai benefits from economies of scale and strong supplier relationships, enabling it to introduce competitive pricing strategies that smaller rivals in the 油污净 segment cannot easily match.

## 蓝月亮（中国）有限公司 (Blue Moon (China) Co., Ltd.)

Blue Moon (China) Co., Ltd. is a leading player in the Chinese household cleaning products market, backed by significant foreign investment (registered capital of USD 170 million) and a Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan legal structure that brings international management standards and capital strength.

**Innovation and Technology Leadership:** Blue Moon's competitive strength in the 油污净 industry is grounded in its commitment to research and development. Its business scope explicitly includes engineering and technology research, new materials technology promotion, and household appliance R&D. This positions Blue Moon as a technology-driven company capable of developing proprietary, high-performance oil-removal formulas that stand out in efficacy tests and consumer reviews, creating a strong product differentiation advantage.

**Premium Brand Positioning:** Unlike more price-sensitive competitors, Blue Moon has successfully cultivated a premium brand image, particularly among urban consumers. In the 油污净 segment, this allows the company to command higher price points while maintaining strong consumer loyalty. Its marketing investments in digital and social media channels reinforce brand awareness among younger, higher-income demographics who prioritize cleaning performance.

**Advanced Digital and E-Commerce Capabilities:** Blue Moon's registered scope includes internet sales, individual internet live-streaming services, online data processing, and value-added telecom services. This digital-first approach has been highly effective in modern China's retail landscape, enabling the company to engage consumers directly through platforms such as Tmall, JD.com, and live-streaming commerce—driving strong sales volumes in the 油污净 category.

**Comprehensive Service Ecosystem:** Blue Moon extends beyond product manufacturing into professional cleaning services, including specialized cleaning, disinfection, and household services. This service ecosystem reinforces its brand authority in cleaning solutions and creates additional touchpoints that build consumer trust in its 油污净 product line.

**International Capital and Governance:** With USD-denominated registered capital and international corporate governance standards, Blue Moon has the financial strength to invest in large-scale marketing campaigns, product innovation cycles, and supply chain optimization—all critical to sustaining competitiveness in the 油污净 market.
